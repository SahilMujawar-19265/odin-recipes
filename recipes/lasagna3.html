Cheese Lasagna

This cheese lasagna recipe is an easy vegetarian lasagna with ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheeses.

Ingredients

  • lasagna noodles
  • ricotta cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • spaghetti sauce
  • Italian seasoning
  • mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C)
  2. Add lasagna noodles and cook for 8 to 10 minutes
  3. Mix ricotta,Parmesan cheese, salt, and pepper together in a bowl
  4. add garlic and sauté 2 minutes.
  5. Stir in spaghetti sauce and Italian seasoning
  6. Cover with a greased sheet of foil.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted and bubbly
  8. Serve and enjoy!
