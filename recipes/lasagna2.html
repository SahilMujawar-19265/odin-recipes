Creamy Chicken Lasagna

This chicken lasagna is made with tender chicken breasts, red sauce, and lots of mozzarella cheese for a fantastic flavor combination. Everyone who tastes it raves!

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • lasagna noodles
  • mozzarella cheese
  • package cream
  • spaghetti sauce
  • Mint Leaves

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles in boiling water.
  3. Drain, rinse with cold water, and set aside.
  4. Cook until chicken is no longer pink and the juices run clear, about 20 minutes.
  5. Combine shredded chicken, 1 cup mozzarella cheese, and cream cheese in a large bowl.
  6. Spread 1/3 of the spaghetti sauce in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes.
  8. Serve and enjoy!
