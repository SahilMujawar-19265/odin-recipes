Chicken Lasagna

Making perfect homemade lasagna doesn’t have to be tedious.

This top-rated easy lasagna recipe comes together quickly with a relatively short ingredient list.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Seasonings
  • Eggs
  • Cheeses
  • Spaghetti sauce

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients and preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Cook and stir ground Chicken in the hot skillet until browned and crumbly,10 minutes.
  3. Drain and discard grease. Stir in spaghetti sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Combine cottage cheese, 2 cups of mozzarella cheese, eggs, 1/2 of the grated Parmesan cheese, dried parsley, salt, and pepper in a large bowl.
  5. Spread 3/4 cup of sauce in a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
